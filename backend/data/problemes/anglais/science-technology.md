### Problème 1 : The advantages and drawbacks of technology
\begin{enonce}
Write a short paragraph of about 80 words on the topic: *"Modern technology: a help or a danger for young people?"* Mention at least two advantages and two drawbacks. Use some of these words: device, innovation, screen time, connect, distract.

Follow this plan:
\begin{itemize}
    \item One opening sentence that gives your general idea.
    \item Two advantages.
    \item Two drawbacks.
    \item One closing sentence with a balanced opinion.
\end{itemize}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph (your own words may differ):

*"Modern technology has changed the daily life of young people in many ways. On the one hand, every new **device** helps students learn faster and **connect** with friends and teachers around the world. **Innovation** also opens doors to jobs that did not exist before. On the other hand, too much **screen time** can harm our health and **distract** us from our studies. It can also make some teenagers feel lonely. In my view, technology is useful only when we use it wisely and in moderation."*

Notice the clear structure: a topic sentence, *on the one hand* for advantages, *on the other hand* for drawbacks, and a balanced conclusion.

### Problème 2 : An opinion paragraph about the environment
\begin{enonce}
Some people say that protecting the environment is the job of governments, not ordinary citizens. Write a paragraph of about 90 words giving your own opinion. Support it with two or three examples of what a single person can do.

Useful expressions: *I strongly believe that..., for example..., as a result..., in conclusion...*
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph:

*"I strongly believe that protecting the environment is everyone's responsibility, not only the government's. Of course, big decisions about factories and renewable energy come from the state, but small daily actions matter too. For example, each of us can recycle paper and plastic, save water, and walk or cycle for short journeys. As a result, we reduce pollution and our own carbon footprint. If millions of citizens act together, the effect becomes huge. In conclusion, real change starts with the choices we make at home every single day."*

The paragraph states a clear opinion, gives concrete examples, and ends with a strong conclusion, while reusing the theme vocabulary (recycle, pollution, carbon footprint, renewable energy).

### Problème 3 : Reuse the lexicon — a guided text
\begin{enonce}
Write a short text of 6 to 8 sentences describing a green project for your school or town. You must use at least six of the following words correctly:

(sustainable, recycling, renewable energy, pollution, device, innovation, waste, awareness)
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model text (your sentences and ideas should be original):

*"Last month, our school started a new and **sustainable** project to make our building greener. First, we placed **recycling** bins in every classroom so that students can sort their **waste** easily. The science club designed a small solar **device** that uses **renewable energy** to charge phones in the yard. We also organised a campaign to raise **awareness** about air **pollution** in our town. This kind of **innovation** shows that young people can protect the planet. Step by step, our small actions are making a real difference."*

Each highlighted word is used in a correct and natural context. A good answer keeps the sentences simple, links them with words like *first, also,* and *step by step,* and stays on the topic of a green project.

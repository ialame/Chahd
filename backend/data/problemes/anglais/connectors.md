### Problème 1 : Link the sentences of a short paragraph

\begin{enonce}
The following short paragraph is made of separate, choppy sentences. Rewrite it as a smooth text by joining the ideas with a variety of connectors (cause, contrast, addition, consequence, purpose). You may add or remove a few words.

*"Karim wanted to study abroad. His family did not have much money. He worked every weekend in a small shop. He saved enough for the first year. He was accepted by a good university. He still felt a little afraid of living far from home. He decided to go. He wanted to build a better future."*
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A possible connected version (your wording may differ):
*"Karim wanted to study abroad; **however**, his family did not have much money. **Therefore**, he worked every weekend in a small shop **so that** he could save enough for the first year. **As a result**, he was accepted by a good university. **Although** he still felt a little afraid of living far from home, he decided to go **in order to** build a better future."*

Notice how the connectors turn a list of facts into a logical story: contrast (*however*, *although*), consequence (*therefore*, *as a result*), and purpose (*so that*, *in order to*).

### Problème 2 : Improve the cohesion of a text

\begin{enonce}
This paragraph repeats *and* and *but* too often, which makes it sound flat. Rewrite it using more precise connectors so that the relationships between ideas are clear. Keep the same meaning.

*"The internet is very useful and it gives us a lot of information and we can study online and it can be dangerous and some sites are not reliable but we should be careful and we should check our sources and we will use it well."*
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A more cohesive version (your choices may vary):
*"The internet is very useful **because** it gives us a lot of information. **Moreover**, we can study online. **However**, it can also be dangerous, **since** some sites are not reliable. **Therefore**, we should be careful **and** check our sources **so that** we will use it well."*

The improved text replaces the repeated *and / but* with connectors of cause (*because*, *since*), addition (*moreover*), contrast (*however*), consequence (*therefore*), and purpose (*so that*). This makes each sentence relate clearly to the next.

### Problème 3 : Advantages and disadvantages with connectors

\begin{enonce}
Write a short paragraph of 5 to 7 sentences about the **advantages and disadvantages of mobile phones for students**. You must use at least five different connectors, including one of addition, one of contrast, one of cause or consequence, and one of purpose. Underline or put in bold every connector you use.
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph (your own ideas and connectors are welcome):
*"Mobile phones offer many advantages to students. **First of all**, they make communication with friends and family easy. **In addition**, students can use them **in order to** look up information quickly during their studies. **However**, these devices also have serious disadvantages. **For example**, they can distract learners in class, **and consequently** their marks may drop. **On the other hand**, a phone can become useful again **if** it is used responsibly. **To sum up**, mobile phones are helpful tools, **but** students should control their use **so that** they do not lose focus."*

The paragraph is organised with connectors of order (*first of all*, *to sum up*), addition (*in addition*), illustration (*for example*), contrast (*however*, *on the other hand*, *but*), consequence (*consequently*), and purpose (*in order to*, *so that*). This variety gives the text a clear and balanced structure.

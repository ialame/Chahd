### Problème 1 : Causes and consequences of the brain drain
\begin{enonce}
Write a paragraph of about 80 to 100 words explaining the main causes and consequences of the brain drain in developing countries. Use at least five of these words: *brain drain, qualifications, opportunities, abroad, unemployment, expertise, developing*.
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph (your own wording may differ):

*"The **brain drain** happens when highly educated people leave their **developing** country to work **abroad**. There are several causes. Many graduates with strong **qualifications** cannot find suitable jobs at home, and high **unemployment** pushes them to look for better **opportunities** elsewhere. Low salaries and poor working conditions make the situation worse. The consequences are serious: the country loses the **expertise** of its best doctors, engineers and teachers. As a result, hospitals, schools and companies suffer, and the gap between rich and poor nations keeps growing."*

Notice how each themed word is used in context, and how the paragraph is clearly divided into **causes** and **consequences**.

### Problème 2 : Opinion paragraph — youth unemployment
\begin{enonce}
Some people say that young people should accept any job, even one far from their studies, while others believe they should wait for a job that matches their **qualifications**. Write a short opinion paragraph (about 80 words). Give your point of view and two reasons, and use the words *unemployment*, *skills* and *opportunity*.
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model answer (one possible opinion):

*"In my opinion, young people should accept a first job even if it is not perfect. With high **unemployment**, waiting for the ideal position can waste valuable years. First, any job allows graduates to gain experience and develop new **skills** that employers value. Second, a modest job can become an **opportunity** to meet people and discover a better career later. Of course, the salary may be low at the beginning, but it is wiser to start working than to stay at home doing nothing."*

A clear structure (opinion + two reasons + a small concession) makes the paragraph convincing.

### Problème 3 : Using the lexis — international cooperation
\begin{enonce}
Imagine your school takes part in an international project with a partner school in another country. Write a short paragraph (about 70 to 90 words) describing the project and how it helps both countries. You must reuse at least five themed words, for example: *cooperation, expertise, skills, developing, opportunity, share, training*.
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph:

*"Our school has started a project of **cooperation** with a partner school abroad. Together, the two teams **share** ideas through video calls and online lessons. Teachers from the richer country offer **training** and bring their **expertise** in science and technology, while our students bring their energy and creativity. This exchange is a great **opportunity** for everybody. Students from both sides improve their language **skills** and learn to respect other cultures. Such partnerships also help **developing** countries by building knowledge that stays at home instead of disappearing."*

This kind of task shows that the same vocabulary can describe a positive answer to the brain drain: keeping knowledge through cooperation rather than losing it through migration.

### Problème 1 : Gap-fill — A student's daily routine
\begin{enonce}
Complete this short text about a student's day. Put each verb in brackets into the correct tense (present simple, present continuous, past simple, past continuous or present perfect).

*"Every weekday, Karima ___ (1: wake) up at six o'clock. She usually ___ (2: prepare) a quick breakfast and then ___ (3: leave) for school. Right now it is eight in the evening, and she ___ (4: sit) at her desk. She ___ (5: already / finish) her maths homework, but she ___ (6: still / work) on her English essay. Yesterday she ___ (7: feel) tired because she ___ (8: study) until very late the night before."*
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **wakes** (a daily habit — *every weekday*)
    \item **prepares** (habit — *usually*)
    \item **leaves** (habit)
    \item **is sitting** (happening now — *right now*)
    \item **has already finished** (present perfect with *already*)
    \item **is still working** (an action in progress now)
    \item **felt** (a finished moment — *yesterday*)
    \item **had been studying / was studying** (a longer action in the past that explains why she felt tired)
\end{enumerate}

### Problème 2 : Rewrite a text with consistent tenses
\begin{enonce}
The story below mixes the tenses in a confusing way. Rewrite the whole paragraph so that it is told entirely in the **past**, keeping the difference between completed actions (past simple) and background actions in progress (past continuous).

*"It is a cold Sunday morning. The wind blows and the streets are empty. Omar walks to the bakery to buy bread. While he is crossing the square, he sees an old man who is feeding the pigeons. He stops, smiles, and decides to help him."*
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model rewrite in the past:
*"It **was** a cold Sunday morning. The wind **was blowing** and the streets **were** empty. Omar **walked** to the bakery to buy bread. While he **was crossing** the square, he **saw** an old man who **was feeding** the pigeons. He **stopped**, **smiled**, and **decided** to help him."*

Notice that the descriptions and background actions (*was blowing*, *was crossing*, *was feeding*) take the **past continuous**, while the main completed events (*walked*, *saw*, *stopped*, *smiled*, *decided*) take the **past simple**.

### Problème 3 : Guided writing — Then and now
\begin{enonce}
Write a short paragraph of five to six sentences comparing your life two years ago with your life today. Use the notes below and choose the right tense for each idea.
\begin{itemize}
    \item where you lived two years ago (past simple)
    \item what you used to do every day back then (past simple)
    \item where you live now and your daily routine (present simple)
    \item what you are doing these days to prepare for the exam (present continuous)
    \item something you have achieved up to now (present perfect)
\end{itemize}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph (your own answer may differ, but the tenses should match):
*"Two years ago, I **lived** in a small village near the mountains. Every morning I **walked** for half an hour to reach my school. Now I **live** in the city, and I **take** the bus to my high school every day. These days I **am preparing** for my final exam, so I **am revising** all my old lessons. Since September, I **have improved** my English a lot, and I **have already finished** three full practice papers."*

The past simple describes the finished period (*two years ago*), the present simple describes today's routine, the present continuous describes temporary actions happening now, and the present perfect connects past efforts to the present result.

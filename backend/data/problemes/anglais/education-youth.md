### Problème 1 : Write a paragraph reusing the vocabulary
\begin{enonce}
Write a short paragraph of about 60 to 80 words on the topic: **"Why education matters for young people."** You must reuse at least five words from this list, underlined or in bold when you use them.

opportunities — skills — confidence — lifelong learning — scholarship — mentor — potential
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph (your own wording may differ):

*"Education matters because it gives young people the **skills** they need to build a better life. A good school offers real **opportunities** to discover one's hidden **potential**, while a patient **mentor** helps each student grow with **confidence**. For talented but poor students, a **scholarship** can change everything. In the end, education is not only about exams: it is about **lifelong learning** and becoming a useful, independent adult."*

This answer is correct because it stays on topic, uses six target words naturally, and forms one clear, connected paragraph.

### Problème 2 : Guided opinion paragraph (for / against)
\begin{enonce}
Some people think that learning a trade through **vocational training** is better for young people than following long academic studies. Others disagree.

Write a guided opinion paragraph (about 80 words). Follow this plan:
\begin{itemize}
    \item State your opinion clearly in the first sentence.
    \item Give two arguments to support it (use words such as *skills*, *job market*, *motivation*).
    \item Mention one argument from the other side and answer it.
    \item End with a short conclusion.
\end{itemize}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph (other opinions are accepted if they are well argued):

*"In my opinion, **vocational training** is a very good choice for many young people. First, it teaches practical **skills** that are directly useful in the **job market**, so graduates find work quickly. Second, students who learn a trade they love often feel more **motivation** than those stuck in subjects they dislike. Some people say that long academic studies open more doors, and that is true for certain careers. However, society needs skilled workers just as much as it needs scholars. Therefore, both paths deserve the same respect."*

This is a strong answer because it announces a clear position, gives two reasons, replies to the opposite view, and closes with a balanced conclusion.

### Problème 3 : From notes to a connected text
\begin{enonce}
Here are some notes about a young student. Use them to write a short connected paragraph of 5 to 6 sentences. Add linking words and reuse the vocabulary of education and youth.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Amine — a gifted but shy teenager from a small village.
    \item Almost dropped out of school because his family had little money.
    \item A caring teacher noticed his potential and became his mentor.
    \item Helped him win a scholarship to a good high school in the city.
    \item Today — studies engineering and dreams of helping his village.
\end{itemize}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph:

*"Amine is a **gifted** but shy teenager from a small village. Because his family had very little money, he almost **dropped out** of school. Fortunately, a caring teacher noticed his real **potential** and decided to become his **mentor**. Thanks to this guidance, Amine managed to win a **scholarship** to a good high school in the city. Today, he studies engineering, and he dreams of using his knowledge to help the children of his own village."*

This answer is correct because every note is turned into a full sentence, the events are linked with words like *because*, *fortunately* and *thanks to*, and the key vocabulary is reused naturally.

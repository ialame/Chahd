### Problème 1 : Rewrite a connected text in the passive
\begin{enonce}
The following short report is written in the active voice. Rewrite the whole text in the passive voice. Drop the agent when it is not important.

*"Every month, the students produce a school magazine. They write the articles, an art teacher checks the drawings, and the head teacher approves the final version. The school prints two hundred copies and sells them at a low price. They use the money to plant trees in the schoolyard."*
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A possible passive version (agents kept only when useful):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Every month, a school magazine **is produced** (by the students).
    \item The articles **are written**, the drawings **are checked** by an art teacher, and the final version **is approved** by the head teacher.
    \item Two hundred copies **are printed** and **are sold** at a low price.
    \item The money **is used** to plant trees in the schoolyard.
\end{enumerate}
Notice that the passive keeps the focus on the magazine and the actions, which makes the report sound more impersonal and formal.

### Problème 2 : Gap-fill — How argan oil is made
\begin{enonce}
Complete this short text about a traditional process. Put each verb in brackets into the correct **active** or **passive** form (present simple).

*"Argan oil ___ (1: produce) in the south-west of Morocco. First, the fruit ___ (2: collect) by women from the cooperatives. The hard nuts ___ (3: dry) in the sun, and then they ___ (4: crack) by hand. The kernels inside ___ (5: contain) the oil. Finally, the women ___ (6: press) the kernels, and the precious oil ___ (7: sell) all over the world."*
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
Use the **passive** when the focus is on the product and the doer is general; use the **active** when the subject (the women, the kernels) does the action.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item is produced (passive — focus on the oil)
    \item is collected (passive, *by women*)
    \item are dried (passive)
    \item are cracked (passive, *by hand*)
    \item contain (active — *the kernels* is the doer)
    \item press (active — *the women* is the doer)
    \item is sold (passive — focus on the oil)
\end{enumerate}

### Problème 3 : From active notes to a formal passive paragraph
\begin{enonce}
Here are some notes about a new public library (active voice). Use them to write a short, formal paragraph of 4 to 5 sentences in the **passive voice**, as if for an official announcement.
\begin{itemize}
    \item The town built a new library last year.
    \item Architects designed it in a modern style.
    \item The town has equipped it with thousands of books and computers.
    \item Volunteers organise reading activities for children every weekend.
    \item People can borrow up to five books at a time.
\end{itemize}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph:
*"A new public library **was built** in the town last year. It **was designed** in a modern style and **has been equipped** with thousands of books and computers. Reading activities for children **are organised** every weekend. Up to five books **can be borrowed** at a time."*

Each sentence focuses on the library and its services rather than on the people involved, which is typical of formal, official writing.

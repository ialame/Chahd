### Problème 1 : Preserving cultural identity
\begin{enonce}
Write a short paragraph of about 60 to 80 words on the following topic:

*"How can young people preserve their cultural identity in a globalized world?"*

In your paragraph, you must use at least four of these words: heritage, identity, traditions, values, globalization, diversity.
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph (the four target words are in **bold**):

*"In a world shaped by **globalization**, young people can still protect their cultural **identity** in simple ways. They can learn their mother tongue, take part in local festivals, and respect family **values** such as hospitality and solidarity. By keeping their **traditions** alive and teaching them to younger children, they make sure that their **heritage** is not forgotten. Being modern and being proud of one's culture are not opposites; the two can live together."*

Notice how the paragraph gives concrete examples (language, festivals, values) instead of staying general.

### Problème 2 : Opinion paragraph on globalization
\begin{enonce}
Globalization has both advantages and risks for local cultures. Write a paragraph of about 80 words giving your opinion. Mention at least one advantage and one risk, and end with a clear personal position.

Use linking words such as *on the one hand, on the other hand, however, in my opinion*.
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A model paragraph:

*"In my opinion, globalization is both a chance and a danger for our cultures. On the one hand, it allows a rich cultural exchange: we discover new ideas, foreign films and useful technologies. On the other hand, it can weaken local traditions, because many young people start to imitate the same global lifestyle and forget their own customs. However, I believe we can enjoy the benefits of an open world while still protecting our identity and our heritage. Balance is the key."*

The paragraph follows a clear plan: advantage, risk, then a balanced personal conclusion.

### Problème 3 : Using the new vocabulary
\begin{enonce}
Write five sentences of your own about cultural values and globalization. Each sentence must contain one of the following words, used correctly: stereotype, multiculturalism, custom, diversity, cultural exchange.
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A set of model sentences (one target word in **bold** each time):

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Judging a whole nation by one bad film is just a harmful **stereotype**.
    \item Big cities like Casablanca are good examples of peaceful **multiculturalism**.
    \item Offering tea to a guest is a warm Moroccan **custom**.
    \item Our class is full of **diversity**, with students from many regions and backgrounds.
    \item A school trip abroad is a real **cultural exchange** that helps us understand others.
\end{enumerate}

Each sentence shows the meaning of the word through a clear, concrete situation, which is exactly what an examiner looks for.

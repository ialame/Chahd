**How Travelling Helps Young People Grow**

Travelling is one of the most rewarding experiences a young person can have. **First of all**, it opens the mind to new cultures: meeting people who live and think differently teaches us tolerance and respect for diversity. **In addition**, travelling builds independence, because young travellers must plan their journeys, manage a budget and solve problems on their own. **Furthermore**, it is a powerful way to learn languages, since practising with native speakers is far more effective than studying from a book. **Finally**, exploring new places creates unforgettable memories and helps us see our own country with fresh eyes. **In short**, every young person should travel whenever possible, because it turns the whole world into a classroom.

Nowadays, more and more people move to big cities hoping for a better life, yet city living comes with serious problems. **The first** and most obvious one is traffic: roads are crowded, buses are packed, and people waste long hours commuting every single day. **Another** major issue is the high cost of living, since rents, food and basic services are far more expensive than in small towns, which puts heavy pressure on ordinary families. **In addition**, big cities suffer from pollution and noise, and the constant rush can leave residents feeling stressed, tired and lonely despite being surrounded by crowds. **To deal with** these challenges, authorities should improve public transport, build more affordable housing and create green spaces where people can relax. **In conclusion**, big cities offer many opportunities, but they will only remain pleasant places to live if their growing problems are taken seriously.

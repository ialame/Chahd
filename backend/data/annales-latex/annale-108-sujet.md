**Task 2 — Article: social and economic problems Moroccans faced because of Covid-19** *(model answer)*

**Living Through the Pandemic: How Covid-19 Changed Our Lives**

The Covid-19 pandemic affected the whole world, and Morocco was no exception. **On the economic side**, the lockdown forced many shops, cafés and factories to close, so a large number of people lost their jobs or saw their incomes fall sharply. Small businesses and daily workers, who depend on going out to earn a living, were hit the hardest, and tourism — a key source of income for many families — almost stopped completely.

**On the social side**, the crisis was just as difficult. Schools and universities closed, and many students struggled to follow online lessons because they lacked computers or a good internet connection. Long periods of isolation also affected people's mental health, and families could no longer gather as they used to.

**In short**, the pandemic left deep marks on Moroccan society and economy. However, it also reminded us of the importance of solidarity, and it showed how, by working together, we can face even the hardest challenges.

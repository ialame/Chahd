**Task 2 — Article: "Ways to keep your neighbourhood clean."** *(model answer)*

**Keeping Our Neighbourhood Clean: A Shared Duty**

Recently, I have noticed that our neighbourhood is becoming dirtier, with litter on the pavements and overflowing bins. This is a serious problem, but together we can solve it.

First of all, every family should sort its rubbish and throw it only in the proper containers, never in the street. In addition, the local authorities should place more bins on the corners and empty them regularly. We could also organise weekend clean-up campaigns where neighbours, especially young volunteers, gather to collect waste and plant a few trees. Finally, raising awareness through posters and school activities would teach children that a clean environment is everyone's responsibility.

If we all play our part, our neighbourhood will become a healthier and more pleasant place to live. A clean street is the first sign of a community that respects itself.

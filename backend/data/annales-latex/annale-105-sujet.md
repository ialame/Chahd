**Task 2 — Article: causes, consequences and solutions of disappearing green areas** *(model answer)*

**Where Have Our Green Spaces Gone?**

In our region, the parks, forests and green areas that we once took for granted are vanishing at an alarming speed. As a student who cares about the future, I believe we must understand this problem before it is too late.

There are several **causes** behind this phenomenon. The fast growth of cities pushes builders to cut down trees in order to make room for houses, roads and shopping centres. At the same time, illegal logging, careless waste dumping and frequent fires destroy what little greenery remains. A general lack of awareness only makes things worse.

The **consequences** are serious. Without trees, the air becomes more polluted and temperatures rise, which harms our health. Many birds and animals lose their natural homes, and families lose the quiet, healthy places where they used to relax and exercise.

Fortunately, **solutions** do exist. Local authorities should plant more trees and protect existing forests through strict laws. Schools can launch awareness campaigns and tree-planting days, while every citizen can help by reducing waste and respecting public parks. If we all act together, we can keep our region green for the generations to come.

**Task 2 — Funny story for the school competition** *(model answer)*

Last summer, my family decided to surprise my grandmother on her birthday. We hid in the dark living room, holding a big cake, waiting for her to walk in so we could shout "Surprise!" The problem was that my little brother could not stop giggling, and my uncle kept whispering at him to be quiet. When the door finally opened, we all jumped up at once and yelled as loudly as we could.

Unfortunately, it was not my grandmother at the door. It was the new neighbour, who had simply come to ask for some sugar. The poor man was so frightened that he dropped his cup, screamed louder than all of us, and ran straight back to his house. We were left frozen, holding a cake in front of an empty doorway.

A few seconds later, my grandmother arrived from the kitchen, where she had been the whole time, calmly asking why we were shouting at the neighbour. Everyone burst out laughing, and even the embarrassed neighbour came back to share the cake. To this day, whenever someone knocks on our door, my brother shouts "Surprise!" just to make us laugh again.

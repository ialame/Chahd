**Travelling Together: Why I Choose My Family**

When it comes to travelling, people are often divided between going with friends and going with family. Personally, I prefer to travel with my family, and I have good reasons for this choice.

**First of all**, travelling with my family makes me feel safe and relaxed. My parents take care of the planning and the budget, so I can simply enjoy the journey without worrying about every detail. **Moreover**, a family trip is a precious chance to spend quality time together, far from our busy daily routine; we talk, laugh, and create memories that last for years.

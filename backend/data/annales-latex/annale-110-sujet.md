**Task 2 — Article: causes of violence in schools in Morocco** *(model answer)*

**School Violence: Where Does It Come From?**

Violence in schools has unfortunately become a serious problem in Morocco, and understanding its causes is the first step towards a solution. **One major cause** is the family environment: children who grow up surrounded by conflict, neglect, or harsh punishment often copy that aggressive behaviour at school. **A second factor** is the influence of the media and social networks, where young people are constantly exposed to violent images and videos that make fighting look normal or even admirable. **Moreover**, overcrowded classrooms and a lack of guidance counsellors mean that small disagreements are rarely calmed down before they turn into real fights. **Poverty and failure at school** also play a part, since frustrated students who feel they have no future may release their anger on their classmates or teachers. **To conclude**, school violence is not caused by one single reason but by a mix of family, social, and educational problems. Tackling it will require parents, teachers, and society to work together so that schools can once again be safe places to learn.

### Exercice 1 : Gap-fill — the vocabulary of sustainability
\begin{enonce}
Complete each sentence with the correct word from the list. There is one extra word you will not use.

(innovation, renewable, pollution, recycling, sustainability, carbon footprint, device)
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Walking to school instead of driving is a simple way to reduce your ___.
    \item Solar and wind power are two well-known sources of ___ energy.
    \item Smoke from factories is one of the main causes of air ___.
    \item ___ turns old bottles and paper into useful materials again.
    \item A smartphone is a small electronic ___ that we use every day.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item carbon footprint — your **carbon footprint** is the amount of CO2 your daily habits produce.
    \item **renewable** — energy that never runs out.
    \item **pollution** — dirty or harmful substances in the air.
    \item **Recycling** — the process of reusing waste materials.
    \item **device** — a piece of equipment made for a purpose.
\end{enumerate}
The extra word is *innovation* and *sustainability*.

### Exercice 2 : Word formation — nouns and adjectives
\begin{enonce}
Put the word in brackets into the correct form to complete each sentence.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Burning coal causes a lot of ___. (pollute)
    \item This factory has made several ___ improvements. (technology)
    \item We must protect the planet for the sake of ___. (sustainable)
    \item A good engineer is always full of ___ ideas. (innovate)
    \item The new engine is far more ___ than the old one. (energy)
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **pollution** (verb to noun)
    \item **technological** (noun to adjective)
    \item **sustainability** (adjective to noun)
    \item **innovative** (verb to adjective)
    \item **energy-efficient** (compound adjective; *efficient* is also accepted)
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 3 : Match the word to its definition
\begin{enonce}
Match each word (1 to 6) with its correct definition (a to f).
\begin{enumerate}
    \item innovation
    \item recycling
    \item drought
    \item greenhouse gas
    \item habitat
    \item efficiency
\end{enumerate}
Definitions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item a. the natural home of a plant or an animal
    \item b. a long period without rain
    \item c. a new idea, method, or product
    \item d. a gas that traps heat and warms the planet
    \item e. doing a task without wasting time or energy
    \item f. treating used materials so they can be used again
\end{itemize}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item innovation — **c** (a new idea, method, or product)
    \item recycling — **f** (treating used materials so they can be used again)
    \item drought — **b** (a long period without rain)
    \item greenhouse gas — **d** (a gas that traps heat and warms the planet)
    \item habitat — **a** (the natural home of a plant or an animal)
    \item efficiency — **e** (doing a task without wasting time or energy)
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 4 : Choose the correct word
\begin{enonce}
Circle the word that best completes each sentence.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Many cities suffer from heavy traffic and air (pollution / population).
    \item Scientists are looking for clean, (renewable / removable) sources of power.
    \item This phone is the latest (invention / intention) of a young Moroccan engineer.
    \item We should (waste / reduce) the amount of plastic we throw away.
    \item Electric cars produce far fewer (emissions / decisions) than petrol cars.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **pollution** (population means the number of people)
    \item **renewable** (removable means it can be taken off)
    \item **invention** (intention means a plan or aim)
    \item **reduce** (we want less plastic, not more waste)
    \item **emissions** (gases released into the air; decisions are choices)
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 5 : Collocations — find the partner
\begin{enonce}
Match each verb or word on the left with the word it usually goes with on the right.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item reduce
    \item climate
    \item save
    \item cutting-edge
    \item renewable
\end{enumerate}
Right column:
\begin{itemize}
    \item a. energy
    \item b. waste
    \item c. technology
    \item d. change
    \item e. resources
\end{itemize}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item reduce + **b** (reduce **waste**)
    \item climate + **d** (climate **change**)
    \item save + **e** (save **resources**)
    \item cutting-edge + **c** (cutting-edge **technology**, meaning very modern)
    \item renewable + **a** (renewable **energy**)
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 7 : Odd one out
\begin{enonce}
In each line, three words belong to the same topic and one does not. Find the word that does not belong and explain why.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item solar — wind — coal — hydroelectric
    \item smartphone — laptop — tablet — riverbank
    \item pollution — drought — flood — harvest
    \item recycle — reuse — reduce — waste
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **coal** — the others are renewable energy sources; coal is a fossil fuel.
    \item **riverbank** — the others are electronic devices.
    \item **harvest** — the others are environmental problems; a harvest is positive.
    \item **waste** — the others are green actions ("the three Rs"); waste is the opposite.
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 8 : Translate the idea (English to English)
\begin{enonce}
Replace the underlined part of each sentence with a single word from the theme that has the same meaning.

(sustainability, innovation, pollution, recycling)
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Factories must stop releasing harmful substances into the air and water.
    \item The company is famous for its new and original ideas.
    \item Using resources carefully so future generations still have them is very important.
    \item Collecting used paper and glass to make new products saves materials.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **pollution** ("releasing harmful substances")
    \item **innovation** ("new and original ideas")
    \item **sustainability** ("using resources carefully for the future")
    \item **recycling** ("collecting used materials to make new products")
\end{enumerate}

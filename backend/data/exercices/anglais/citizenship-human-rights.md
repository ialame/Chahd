### Exercice 1 : Gap-fill — the key words of citizenship
\begin{enonce}
Complete each sentence with the correct word from the list. Use each word only once.

*rights, duties, equality, democracy, freedom of speech, discrimination, tolerance, solidarity*
\begin{enumerate}
    \item In a real ___, the people choose their leaders through free and fair elections.
    \item ___ means treating others with respect even when they think differently from you.
    \item ___ allows journalists to criticise the government without fear of being punished.
    \item Paying taxes and obeying the law are among the main ___ of every citizen.
    \item ___ means that women and men receive the same wages for the same work.
    \item When a club refuses members because of their colour, that is a clear case of ___.
    \item During the floods, the whole town showed ___ by sharing food and shelter.
    \item Education and a fair trial are basic human ___ that no government should take away.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **democracy**
    \item **tolerance**
    \item **freedom of speech**
    \item **duties**
    \item **equality**
    \item **discrimination**
    \item **solidarity**
    \item **rights**
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 3 : Match the word to its definition
\begin{enonce}
Match each word (1–6) with the correct definition (a–f).
\begin{enumerate}
    \item citizen
    \item discrimination
    \item solidarity
    \item duty
    \item democracy
    \item tolerance
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}
    \item[a.] something a person must do because the law or morality requires it
    \item[b.] a system in which power belongs to the people through their vote
    \item[c.] a member of a country who has both rights and obligations
    \item[d.] the acceptance of opinions and beliefs different from one's own
    \item[e.] unfair treatment of a person because of their group, sex or religion
    \item[f.] mutual support among people who share the same cause or difficulty
\end{itemize}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item citizen → **c**
    \item discrimination → **e**
    \item solidarity → **f**
    \item duty → **a**
    \item democracy → **b**
    \item tolerance → **d**
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 6 : Odd one out
\begin{enonce}
In each group, find the word that does NOT belong with the others and explain why.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item equality — fairness — justice — discrimination
    \item tolerance — respect — hatred — acceptance
    \item vote — election — democracy — censorship
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item **discrimination** — the other three describe fair treatment, while discrimination means unfair treatment.
    \item **hatred** — the others express openness towards people, while hatred is the opposite of tolerance.
    \item **censorship** — the others belong to a democratic system, while censorship blocks free expression.
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 7 : Complete the paragraph
\begin{enonce}
Fill in the gaps with the most suitable word from the list.

*responsibility, citizen, rights, tolerance, society*
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Every ___ is born free and equal in dignity.
    \item Knowing your ___ is the first step towards defending them.
    \item Living together in a peaceful ___ demands patience and ___.
    \item Voting is a ___ that we should never take lightly.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Every **citizen** is born free and equal in dignity.
    \item Knowing your **rights** is the first step towards defending them.
    \item Living together in a peaceful **society** demands patience and **tolerance**.
    \item Voting is a **responsibility** that we should never take lightly.
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 8 : Correct the wrong word
\begin{enonce}
Each sentence uses one word from the theme incorrectly. Replace it with the right one.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item In a healthy democracy, every citizen has the duty to be heard.
    \item Discrimination means accepting people who are different from us.
    \item Solidarity is the unfair treatment of a minority group.
    \item Freedom of speech is the act of paying your taxes on time.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item ...every citizen has the **right** to be heard. (being heard is a right, not a duty)
    \item **Tolerance** means accepting people who are different from us.
    \item **Discrimination** is the unfair treatment of a minority group.
    \item **Civic duty** (or *paying taxes*) is the act of paying your taxes; freedom of speech is the right to express your ideas.
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 1 : Direct to reported — statements with backshift
\begin{enonce}
Turn these statements into reported speech. Start each one with "She said that...".
\begin{enumerate}
    \item "I am tired."
    \item "We live in Rabat."
    \item "I have finished my homework."
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
After a past reporting verb, the tense moves one step back (**backshift**): present simple goes to past simple, present perfect goes to past perfect, and the pronouns change.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item She said that she **was** tired.
    \item She said that they **lived** in Rabat.
    \item She said that she **had finished** her homework.
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 2 : Backshift with modals and the past
\begin{enonce}
Report these sentences. Begin with "He told me that...".
\begin{enumerate}
    \item "I will call you tomorrow."
    \item "I can swim very well."
    \item "I bought a new phone."
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
*Will* becomes **would**, *can* becomes **could**, and past simple becomes **past perfect**.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item He told me that he **would** call me the next day.
    \item He told me that he **could** swim very well.
    \item He told me that he **had bought** a new phone.
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 3 : Reported wh- questions
\begin{enonce}
Report these wh- questions. Begin with "The teacher asked me...".
\begin{enumerate}
    \item "Where do you live?"
    \item "What are you reading?"
    \item "Why did you arrive late?"
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
In a reported question we keep the **wh- word**, change to **statement word order** (subject before verb), and remove *do/does/did*.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The teacher asked me **where I lived**.
    \item The teacher asked me **what I was reading**.
    \item The teacher asked me **why I had arrived** late.
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 4 : Reported yes/no questions
\begin{enonce}
Report these yes/no questions using "if" or "whether". Begin with "Sara asked me...".
\begin{enumerate}
    \item "Do you like football?"
    \item "Have you seen this film?"
    \item "Are you coming to the party?"
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
A yes/no question is introduced by **if** (or **whether**), with statement word order and no *do/does/did*.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Sara asked me **if I liked** football.
    \item Sara asked me **if I had seen** that film.
    \item Sara asked me **whether I was coming** to the party.
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 5 : Reported commands and requests
\begin{enonce}
Report these orders and requests using "tell" or "ask" + to-infinitive.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The coach to the players: "Run faster!"
    \item My mother to me: "Don't open the window."
    \item A tourist to me: "Please help me, can you?"
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
For an order we use **told + object + to + infinitive**; for a request we use **asked**. A negative command uses **not to**.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The coach **told the players to run** faster.
    \item My mother **told me not to open** the window.
    \item A tourist **asked me to help** him.
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 6 : Changing time and place markers
\begin{enonce}
Report these sentences and change the underlined time or place words correctly. Begin with "He said that...".
\begin{enumerate}
    \item "I am busy now."
    \item "I will travel tomorrow."
    \item "I met her here yesterday."
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
When the reporting verb is in the past, markers shift: *now* becomes **then**, *tomorrow* becomes **the next day**, *here* becomes **there**, and *yesterday* becomes **the day before**.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item He said that he was busy **then**.
    \item He said that he would travel **the next day**.
    \item He said that he had met her **there the day before**.
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 7 : Correct the mistake
\begin{enonce}
Each reported sentence contains one mistake. Correct it.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item She said me that she was happy.
    \item He asked me where did I go.
    \item The teacher told us close our books.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
Watch the reporting verb, the word order, and the *to-infinitive*.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item She **told** me that she was happy. (or: She **said** that she was happy — *say* is not followed by an object)
    \item He asked me where **I went**. (statement word order, no *did*)
    \item The teacher told us **to close** our books. (*tell* + object + *to* + infinitive)
\end{enumerate}

### Exercice 8 : Mixed transformation
\begin{enonce}
Report each sentence with the reporting verb given in brackets.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item "I don't understand this lesson." (Karim said)
    \item "When does the bus leave?" (the man asked)
    \item "Turn off your phones, please." (the teacher told us)
\end{enumerate}
\end{enonce}

\textbf{Solution :}
Apply backshift to statements, statement word order to questions, and *to + infinitive* to commands.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Karim said that he **didn't understand** that lesson.
    \item The man asked **when the bus left**.
    \item The teacher told us **to turn off** our phones.
\end{enumerate}

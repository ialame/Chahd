Science and technology shape every part of modern life, from the phones in our pockets to the way we produce energy. But progress also raises a key question for our generation: how can we develop without destroying the planet? This unit builds the **vocabulary** and **ideas** you need to discuss **innovation**, the **environment**, and **sustainable development** in the Bac.

## 1. Key vocabulary

| Word | Meaning / example |
| --- | --- |
| **innovation** | a new idea, method or device. *Solar panels were a major **innovation**.* |
| **artificial intelligence (AI)** | machines that can learn and make decisions like humans. *AI **helps doctors read scans**.* |
| **renewable energy** | energy from sources that never run out (sun, wind, water). *Morocco invests in **renewable energy**.* |
| **fossil fuels** | coal, oil and gas formed underground; they are limited and polluting. |
| **climate change** | long-term changes in the world''s weather, mostly caused by humans. |
| **global warming** | the rise in the Earth''s average temperature. |
| **pollution** | harmful substances in the air, water or soil. *Cars cause air **pollution**.* |
| **recycling** | turning used materials into new products. *We sort waste for **recycling**.* |
| **biodiversity** | the variety of plants and animals in a region. |
| **carbon footprint** | the amount of CO2 a person or activity produces. *Flying increases your **carbon footprint**.* |
| **sustainability** | using resources without harming the future. |
| **breakthrough** | an important discovery or sudden progress. *a medical **breakthrough**.* |
| **device** | a machine made for a purpose (phone, sensor). |
| **data** | information collected and stored, often by computers. |
| **research** | careful study to discover new facts. |
| **scientist** | a person who studies science. |
| **endangered species** | a type of animal at risk of disappearing. |
| **deforestation** | the cutting down of large areas of forest. |
| **greenhouse gases** | gases (like CO2) that trap heat in the atmosphere. |
| **conservation** | protecting nature and natural resources. |

## 2. Useful expressions

\begin{definition}[Talking about technology]
**Advantages:** *Technology **makes life easier**. It **saves time and money**. The Internet **gives access to information**. AI **can solve complex problems**.*
**Disadvantages:** *Technology **can be addictive**. It **reduces face-to-face contact**. Some jobs **may disappear**. There are risks to **privacy and data**.*
\end{definition}

\begin{exemple}
- **On the one hand**, smartphones connect people; **on the other hand**, they can isolate us.
- **One major drawback of** social media is the spread of **fake news**.
- **A clear benefit of** renewable energy is that it **does not pollute**.
\end{exemple}

\begin{remarque}
To discuss the **environment**, use: *We **must protect** the planet. We **should reduce** our waste. It is **high time we acted**. We **ought to save** energy and water.*
\end{remarque}

## 3. Key ideas

\begin{propriete}[Pros and cons of technology]
- **Pros:** faster communication, better medicine, easier access to **education** and information, more efficient industry.
- **Cons:** loss of privacy, dependence on **devices**, possible **unemployment**, and a growing **carbon footprint** from data centres and electronics.
\end{propriete}

\begin{propriete}[Sustainable development]
- **Sustainable development** means meeting the needs of **today without harming future generations**.
- It joins three goals: a **healthy economy**, a **fair society**, and a **protected environment**.
- Examples: using **renewable energy**, **recycling** waste, and building **green cities**.
\end{propriete}

\begin{propriete}[Protecting the planet]
- The main threats are **climate change**, **pollution**, **deforestation** and the loss of **biodiversity**.
- Everyone can act: **save energy**, **reduce, reuse and recycle**, use **public transport**, and **plant trees**.
- Governments must invest in **clean energy** and protect **endangered species**.
\end{propriete}

## Key points

\begin{propriete}[To remember]
- **Innovation** and **artificial intelligence** drive progress, but technology has both **advantages** and **drawbacks**.
- **Renewable energy** replaces **fossil fuels** and helps fight **climate change** and **global warming**.
- **Sustainable development** balances the **economy**, **society** and the **environment** for future generations.
- Protect the planet by reducing your **carbon footprint**, **recycling**, and saving **biodiversity**.
- Use **on the one hand / on the other hand** and **one drawback / a clear benefit** to balance arguments.
\end{propriete}

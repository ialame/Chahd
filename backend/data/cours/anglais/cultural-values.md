Every nation has its own **culture** — the language, beliefs, customs and art that give a people their **identity**. In today's connected world, **globalization** brings cultures closer than ever. This unit reviews the **vocabulary** and **key ideas** you need to discuss **cultural values**, how to **preserve** them, and how to stay **open** to others.

## 1. Key vocabulary

| Word | Meaning / example |
|------|-------------------|
| **culture** | The shared way of life of a group — its language, art and customs. *Moroccan **culture** is rich and varied.* |
| **heritage** | What is passed down from the past: monuments, traditions, knowledge. *We must protect our cultural **heritage**.* |
| **identity** | What makes a person or people unique and recognizable. *Language is part of our national **identity**.* |
| **tradition** | A custom handed down from generation to generation. *Sharing a meal during Eid is a **tradition**.* |
| **custom** | A usual way of behaving in a society. *Removing your shoes at home is a local **custom**.* |
| **value** | A principle that a community considers important. *Hospitality is a core **value** here.* |
| **belief** | Something accepted as true or important by people. *Respect for elders is a deep **belief**.* |
| **multiculturalism** | The presence and acceptance of many cultures in one society. *The city celebrates **multiculturalism**.* |
| **diversity** | The state of having many different people, ideas or cultures together. *Cultural **diversity** makes society richer.* |
| **stereotype** | A fixed, oversimplified idea about a group, often unfair. *We should avoid every **stereotype**.* |
| **globalization** | The process by which countries become economically and culturally connected. *__Globalization__ links distant markets.* |
| **cultural exchange** | The sharing of ideas, art and customs between peoples. *A **cultural exchange** programme sends students abroad.* |
| **ancestor** | A person from whom one is descended, from earlier generations. *Our **ancestors** built these old walls.* |
| **to gain new ground** | To spread and become more widespread or accepted. *English **is gaining new ground** worldwide.* |
| **to preserve** | To keep something safe and unchanged for the future. *Museums **preserve** ancient art.* |
| **to spread** | To reach more places or people. *Fast food has **spread** across the globe.* |
| **open-minded** | Ready to accept new ideas and other ways of life. *A traveller must be **open-minded**.* |
| **roots** | A person's origins, family and cultural background. *He never forgot his **roots**.* |

## 2. Useful expressions

\begin{definition}[Phrases to talk about culture]
- **to be proud of one's heritage** — *I am **proud of my heritage**.*
- **to hand down (a tradition)** — *Recipes are **handed down** from mother to daughter.*
- **to broaden one's horizons** — *Travelling **broadens your horizons**.*
- **to bridge the gap between cultures** — *Music can **bridge the gap between cultures**.*
- **to lose touch with one's roots** — *Living abroad, he feared **losing touch with his roots**.*
- **to embrace diversity** — *Modern societies should **embrace diversity**.*
\end{definition}

\begin{exemple}
- **On the one hand**, globalization spreads useful ideas; **on the other hand**, it can threaten local traditions.
- **In my opinion**, we can be modern **without** giving up our cultural identity.
\end{exemple}

## 3. Key ideas

\begin{propriete}[Preserving cultural identity]
- A people's **identity** lives in its **language**, **traditions** and **values**.
- To **preserve** this heritage, we **hand down** customs, protect monuments and teach the **mother tongue** to the young.
- Losing one's culture means **losing touch with one's roots**.
\end{propriete}

\begin{propriete}[Openness vs. globalization]
- **Globalization** connects the world: it spreads technology, languages and ideas, and lets cultures **gain new ground**.
- The danger is **uniformity** — when global brands and habits replace local ones.
- The balance: stay **open-minded** and benefit from **cultural exchange**, **without** abandoning your own values.
\end{propriete}

\begin{remarque}
Respecting **diversity** means rejecting every **stereotype**. People are individuals, not labels. **Multiculturalism** works only when each culture is treated with **respect**.
\end{remarque}

## Key points

\begin{propriete}[To remember]
- **Culture** = the shared language, beliefs, customs and art of a people; **identity** is what makes them unique.
- **Heritage** and **traditions** are **handed down** from **ancestors**; we must **preserve** them.
- **Globalization** connects cultures and lets ideas **gain new ground**, but it can threaten local **values**.
- Be **open-minded** and **embrace diversity** through **cultural exchange**, while avoiding every **stereotype**.
\end{propriete}

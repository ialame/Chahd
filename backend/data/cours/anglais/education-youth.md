Education shapes the future of a country, and young people are at the heart of it. This thematic unit gives you the **vocabulary**, **expressions** and **key ideas** you need to speak and write about **education** and the **gifts of youth** in the Bac exam.

## 1. Key vocabulary

| Word | Meaning / example |
|------|-------------------|
| **literacy** | the ability to read and write — *Adult **literacy** programmes help people who never went to school.* |
| **illiteracy** | the inability to read or write — *Fighting **illiteracy** in rural areas is a national priority.* |
| **dropout** | a student who leaves school before finishing — *School **dropouts** often struggle to find good jobs.* |
| **curriculum** | the full set of subjects taught in a school — *The new **curriculum** includes coding and digital skills.* |
| **scholarship** | money given to a talented student to pay for studies — *She won a **scholarship** to study abroad.* |
| **vocational training** | practical training for a specific trade or job — *He chose **vocational training** to become an electrician.* |
| **lifelong learning** | the habit of learning throughout one's whole life — *In a fast-changing world, **lifelong learning** is essential.* |
| **mentor** | an experienced person who guides and advises a younger one — *A good **mentor** can change a teenager's path.* |
| **knowledge** | the facts and understanding a person gains — *Reading widely builds real **knowledge**.* |
| **skills** | the practical abilities you develop with practice — *Soft **skills** like teamwork matter as much as grades.* |
| **motivation** | the inner drive that pushes you to act and study — *Clear goals boost a learner's **motivation**.* |
| **discipline** | the ability to control yourself and work regularly — *Success at school requires daily **discipline**.* |
| **creativity** | the ability to imagine new ideas and solutions — *Art classes nurture **creativity**.* |
| **talent** | a natural ability someone is born with — *Education helps a hidden **talent** grow.* |
| **achievement** | something done successfully through effort — *Passing the Bac is a real **achievement**.* |
| **volunteering** | giving your time freely to help others — *Many students gain experience through **volunteering**.* |
| **tuition fees** | the money paid for teaching at a school or university — *High **tuition fees** can block poorer students.* |
| **graduate** | a person who has completed a degree — *A young **graduate** looks for a first job.* |
| **enrolment** | the act of registering as a student — *Girls' **enrolment** in school keeps rising.* |
| **curiosity** | a strong wish to learn and discover — *Genuine **curiosity** is the engine of learning.* |

## 2. Useful expressions

\begin{exemple}
- to **drop out of** school / to **stay in** school
- to **broaden one's horizons** through education
- to **make the most of** an opportunity
- **education is the key to** a better future
- to **bridge the gap between** rich and poor
- to **raise awareness about** the value of school
- to **unlock** someone's **potential**
- to **acquire knowledge and skills**
- a **thirst for knowledge**
- to **give back to** one's community through **volunteering**
\end{exemple}

## 3. Key ideas

\begin{remarque}
**The importance of education.** Education is far more than passing exams. It develops **critical thinking**, opens the mind, and gives young people the **knowledge** and **skills** they need to build their lives and serve their country.
\end{remarque}

\begin{remarque}
**The challenges of youth.** Many young people face **poverty**, school **dropout**, unemployment, or a lack of **motivation**. Naming these challenges helps you suggest solutions: **scholarships**, **vocational training**, and good **mentors**.
\end{remarque}

\begin{remarque}
**The gifts of youth: creativity and energy.** Young people bring **creativity**, **curiosity** and fresh ideas. When society gives them a chance, their **talent** and energy can drive real change and innovation.
\end{remarque}

\begin{remarque}
**Volunteering and giving back.** Through **volunteering**, young people develop **skills**, gain confidence, and help their community. It is one of the best ways to turn youthful energy into something useful.
\end{remarque}

## Key points

\begin{propriete}[Key points]
- **Education** builds **literacy**, **knowledge** and **skills**, and is the key to a better future.
- A strong learner combines **motivation**, **discipline** and **curiosity**.
- The main challenges of youth are **dropout**, poverty and unemployment; useful answers are **scholarships**, **vocational training** and **mentors**.
- The gifts of youth — **creativity**, **talent** and energy — grow when young people get real opportunities.
- **Volunteering** and **lifelong learning** help young people give back and keep growing.
\end{propriete}

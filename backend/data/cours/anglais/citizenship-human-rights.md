Being a **citizen** means more than living in a country: it means having **rights** and accepting **duties** towards others and towards society. This unit gives you the key **vocabulary**, the **expressions**, and the **ideas** you need to speak and write about **citizenship** and **human rights** for the Bac.

## 1. Key vocabulary

| Word | Meaning / example |
| --- | --- |
| **citizen** | A person who belongs to a country and has its rights and duties. *Every **citizen** has the right to vote.* |
| **citizenship** | The state of being a member of a country, with its rights and responsibilities. *Good **citizenship** means caring for your community.* |
| **rights** | Things a person is morally or legally allowed to have or do. *Education and health are basic **rights**.* |
| **duties** | Things a person is expected to do for society. *Paying taxes is one of our **duties**.* |
| **responsibility** | The duty to deal with something and accept the result of one's actions. *We share the **responsibility** of protecting nature.* |
| **human rights** | The basic freedoms that every person has simply because they are human. *No one should violate **human rights**.* |
| **equality** | The state in which everyone has the same status and opportunities. *We believe in **equality** between men and women.* |
| **justice** | Fair treatment of people according to the law. *A good society is based on **justice**.* |
| **freedom of speech** | The right to express one's opinions freely. *The press needs **freedom of speech**.* |
| **democracy** | A system in which citizens choose their leaders by voting. *In a **democracy**, the people hold the power.* |
| **discrimination** | Unfair treatment of people because of race, gender, or religion. *We must fight **discrimination** in all its forms.* |
| **tolerance** | Acceptance of people whose ideas or ways of life are different. *Living together requires **tolerance**.* |
| **solidarity** | Mutual support among the members of a group or community. *Helping flood victims is an act of **solidarity**.* |
| **dignity** | The quality of being worthy of respect. *Every human being deserves **dignity**.* |
| **respect** | Treating others with consideration and politeness. *Show **respect** for other people's opinions.* |
| **volunteering** | Offering to do useful work without being paid. *She does **volunteering** at a charity.* |
| **NGO** | A non-governmental organization that works for a cause (a charity, an association). *That **NGO** defends children's rights.* |
| **fairness** | The quality of treating people equally and reasonably. *Teachers must judge with **fairness**.* |
| **diversity** | The presence of people of different backgrounds and cultures. *We celebrate the **diversity** of our society.* |
| **community** | A group of people living in the same place or sharing interests. *He works to improve his **community**.* |

## 2. Useful expressions

\begin{exemple}
- **to stand up for one's rights** — *Citizens should **stand up for their rights**.*
- **to raise awareness about** — *The campaign aims **to raise awareness about** discrimination.*
- **to take part in** — *Many young people **take part in** volunteering.*
- **to fight against discrimination** — *Schools teach pupils **to fight against discrimination**.*
- **to obey / break the law** — *A good citizen **obeys the law**.*
- **to make a difference** — *Small actions can **make a difference**.*
- **regardless of race, gender or religion** — *Everyone is equal **regardless of race, gender or religion**.*
\end{exemple}

## 3. Key ideas

\begin{definition}[Being a good citizen]
A good citizen **respects the law**, **votes**, **pays taxes**, helps others, and protects the **environment**. Citizenship is **active**: it means **taking part** in the life of the community, not just enjoying its benefits.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Rights and duties go together]
For every **right** there is a matching **duty**. We have the right to a clean environment, so we have the duty to keep it clean; we have the right to be respected, so we have the duty to **respect** others.
\end{definition}

\begin{remarque}
**Equality** means that all people should have the same **opportunities** and be treated the same way before the **law**, **regardless of** their race, gender, religion, or origin.
\end{remarque}

\begin{propriete}[Fighting discrimination]
- **Discrimination** is treating people unfairly because of who they are.
- We fight it through **education**, **tolerance**, and **respect** for **diversity**.
- **NGOs**, schools, and the media all help to **raise awareness** and defend **human rights**.
\end{propriete}

## Key points

\begin{propriete}[To remember]
- A **citizen** has both **rights** (education, health, freedom of speech) and **duties** (obey the law, pay taxes, help others).
- **Human rights** belong to every person, **regardless of** race, gender, or religion.
- **Equality**, **justice**, and **tolerance** are the foundations of a fair, **democratic** society.
- We fight **discrimination** through **education**, **solidarity**, and **respect** for **dignity** and **diversity**.
- Active citizenship means **taking part**: **volunteering**, joining an **NGO**, and trying to **make a difference**.
\end{propriete}
